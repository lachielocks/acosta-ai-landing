# Safety Features

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 4 September 2026  
**Applies to:** All users of Acosta AI, including consumer accounts and Authorised Users under enterprise arrangements.

*“Safety and ethics are foundational pillars. Explore built-in guardrails for Academic Integrity, Crisis Detection, and the Acosta Guardian wellbeing system.”*

We've built Acosta AI with safety and ethics as foundational pillars. Below is a breakdown of the active protection systems designed to keep students secure, focused, and mentally healthy.

---

### 1. Acosta Guardian
*Built-In Wellbeing & Workload Protection System*

The Guardian Service is a background monitor that looks out for student well-being during long study sessions.
- It tracks session duration, interaction intensity, and signs of study fatigue.
- If burnout is detected, Guardian triggers supportive intervention overlays, suggesting breaks, hydration, or a shift in study methods.

---

### 2. Academic Integrity

Acosta AI is an educational tool designed to foster independent thought.
- **Refuses "Write my essay" commands:** The AI is strictly instructed to reject requests for complete essays or assignments.
- **Socratic Guidance:** Instead of giving answers, it offers outlines, critiques, and guiding questions.
- **Masquerade Detection:** The system recognizes attempts to disguise homework as hobbies or games to bypass rules.
- **Exam Integrity:** Detects and verifies raw exam papers. In Exam Sim mode, we enforce anti-cheat mechanisms by logging tab switches and hiding the context menu to ensure a focused environment.

---

### 3. Crisis Detection

We prioritize the mental health of our users above all else.
- **Recognizes distress:** The AI is trained to recognize signs of self-harm, severe distress, or depressive language in prompts.
- **Supportive overlays:** When a crisis is detected, the system overrides standard responses and triggers supportive intervention overlays.
- **Direct resources:** We provide immediate, direct links to crisis lifelines and mental health support services.

---

### 4. PII Protection

Protecting Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is critical, especially for younger students.
- **Pre-send scanning:** The platform scans messages for highly sensitive data before sending them to the AI models.
- **Data detection:** Automatically detects phone numbers, credit cards, government IDs, and API keys.
- **Redaction:** Offers one-click redaction tools to ensure students don't accidentally leak private information.

---

### 5. Responsible Imagery

Image generation (powered by our AI partners) comes with strict guardrails.
- **SynthID watermarking:** All AI-generated images contain SynthID watermarks to ensure transparency and traceability.
- **Person filter:** Strict filters prevent the generation of deepfakes or likenesses of real individuals.
- **Content blocks:** Absolute blocks on the generation of violent, sexually explicit, or otherwise harmful visual content.

---

### 6. Session Security

To protect your account and data—especially if you are using shared computers in a school library or classroom—Acosta AI features automatic session management.
- **Inactivity Timeout:** We automatically log you out after a period of inactivity.
- **Configurable:** This interval is configurable in your Settings, defaulting to 30 minutes.
